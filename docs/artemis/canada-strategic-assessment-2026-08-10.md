## Executive judgment

Canada's publicly documented Arctic, continental-defence, cyber-resilience, and critical-infrastructure programs create credible discovery opportunities for ClearGlassInc Artemis. They do **not** establish operational readiness, an award, a procurement entitlement, government or NATO endorsement, or revenue for ClearGlass Inc.

The strongest near-term commercial thesis is a software-only, buyer-funded evidence and operations pilot for an existing civilian or dual-use operator: a northern utility, telecom provider, port/airport operator, regulated infrastructure enterprise, prime contractor, or provincial/territorial emergency-management organization. A federal defence-platform sale is a longer-cycle option that requires security eligibility, an approved procurement vehicle, an integration sponsor, and demonstrated interoperability.

No conclusion in this report relies on information published after the reporting date. Because live public-source access was unavailable during execution, this revision makes no factual claim based on publications after 2025-06-09. Items that may have changed between that verification ceiling and the reporting date are explicitly marked **revalidate**.

## Scope and method

The assessment covers the Canadian Arctic and North, North American continental defence, NATO interoperability, Canada–United States security and infrastructure relationships, and relevant allied and industrial supply chains. It compares the present public posture with approximately 2016–2025 where stable public evidence exists.

Each program claim is classified as one of:

- **official policy:** an announced intent or direction;
- **funded plan:** public funding is identified, but availability and program authority still require confirmation;
- **procurement:** a solicitation or contract is publicly identifiable;
- **delivered capability:** an asset or service has been accepted;
- **operationally effective:** supported by exercises, test evidence, readiness reporting, or an accountable authority.

These stages are not interchangeable. Artemis must preserve them as separate ontology states. An announcement is never evidence of fielded capability; a contract is never evidence of operational effectiveness.

### Verification discipline

Every Artemis ingestion run stores source title, publisher, URL, publication and retrieval time, exact supporting passage, document hash, source type, program/lifecycle state, and supersession status. A second authoritative source is required for every material claim unless the claim is explicitly limited to “the responsible authority announced X.” Independent verification means a distinct accountable publisher or an audit/procurement record—not multiple pages repeating one press release.

## The “approximately 2%” issue

The phrase **approximately 2%** is invalid without a denominator and period. The only permissible normalized statement is:

> defence expenditure as defined by NATO, divided by the country's estimated gross domestic product, for the specific calendar year in NATO's annual table.

The 2025 Government of Canada statement instead describes an intention for **fiscal year 2025–26**. Fiscal-year spending, a NATO calendar-year estimate, and a final national-accounts GDP denominator are different measurements. Artemis must store:

```text
metric_name
numerator_amount
numerator_currency
numerator_price_basis
accounting_definition
denominator_name
denominator_value
denominator_revision
period_start
period_end
estimate_or_actual
publisher
publication_version
```

Until the latest NATO table and final outturn identify those fields, this report does not assert that Canada “spends approximately 2%.” Even a verified 2% ratio would measure inputs, not availability, capability, deployability, procurement performance, Arctic coverage, or mission effect.

## Geographic and mission assessment

### Canadian Arctic and Northern Canada

Publicly supportable needs include intermittent connectivity, sparse maintenance capacity, severe-weather operations, long logistics chains, energy and communications dependency, limited local technical staffing, and the need for Indigenous and territorial participation. These conditions favor offline-first software, store-and-forward telemetry, explicit data residency, low-bandwidth interfaces, explainable alerts, and local operating authority.

The design must not frame northern communities as passive sensor hosts. A pilot requires a named northern operator, community or Indigenous governance participation where applicable, data-sovereignty terms, local benefit, support ownership, and an exit/export plan.

### North American continental defence

NORAD modernization establishes a relevant integration environment, not a direct sale. ClearGlassInc Artemis should target bounded enabling functions—evidence management, integration observability, software supply-chain assurance, simulation-data lineage, or infrastructure health—through an accountable program office or prime. Any claim involving classified coverage, system performance, threat tracks, or readiness is **not publicly verifiable** and must be excluded from commercial material.

### NATO interoperability

Interoperability is a testable engineering property: identity federation, data models, message standards, releasability, auditability, deployment compatibility, and exercise outcomes. It is not demonstrated by saying “NATO-grade.” ClearGlassInc Artemis must name the applicable standard/profile, test authority, test result, participating environment, and limitations. Nothing in the evidence register indicates NATO endorsement of ClearGlass Inc.

### Canada–United States relationships

Cross-border missions create requirements for releasability, sovereign policy enforcement, export controls, data location, incident coordination, and supply-chain provenance. They also increase contracting complexity. A Canadian buyer, U.S. stakeholder, binational mission, and procurement authority must not be collapsed into one “government customer.”

## Competitive and delivery risks

- **Platform dependency:** Palantir licensing, deployment rights, APIs, and customer entitlements are unknown until validated. Maintain portable OpenAPI, event, OCI, OTel, and object-storage boundaries.
- **Security eligibility:** controlled or classified work may require organization/personnel clearances, controlled-goods compliance, secure facilities, approved cloud regions, or a prime relationship. These are gating facts, not roadmap details.
- **Procurement duration:** federal opportunities can outlast startup runway. Lead with commercial critical infrastructure and paid subcontracts while qualifying public pathways.
- **Data rights:** operational telemetry, Indigenous data, personal information, export-controlled material, and vendor data require explicit authority and retention terms.
- **Northern operations:** connectivity, maintenance, travel, power, and local capacity can dominate software economics. Do not purchase or own field hardware before a paid, partner-backed validation.
- **Evidence staleness:** all current program, funding, solicitation, NATO percentage, and delivery claims are **revalidate** because the live source search failed at execution.
- **Information boundary:** classified readiness, threat intelligence, commercial bids, vendor pricing, and internal program health are impossible to verify publicly unless lawfully supplied by the owner.

## ClearGlassInc Artemis implementation changes

The intelligence ontology must add `MetricDefinition`, `ProgramMilestone`, `ProcurementMechanism`, `BuyerHypothesis`, `Pilot`, and `VerificationTask`. A `ProgramMilestone` cannot advance from announced to funded, procured, delivered, or operationally effective without evidence allowed for that transition. Revenue scoring refuses records missing buyer, budget path, procurement mechanism, and pilot.

```python
def qualify_opportunity(opportunity: Opportunity) -> Qualification:
    required = {
        "buyer": opportunity.buyer,
        "budget_path": opportunity.budget_path,
        "procurement_mechanism": opportunity.procurement_mechanism,
        "paid_pilot": opportunity.paid_pilot,
        "decision_date": opportunity.decision_date,
        "stop_condition": opportunity.stop_condition,
    }
    missing = tuple(name for name, value in required.items() if not value)
    return Qualification(eligible=not missing, missing=missing, forecast_value=0 if missing else opportunity.risked_value)
```

An assessment release is blocked if a quantitative claim lacks unit, denominator, period, accounting basis, status, and source version; if a readiness claim is supported only by policy/procurement evidence; or if an opportunity lacks the six qualification fields above.
